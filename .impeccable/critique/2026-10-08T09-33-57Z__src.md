## Design specificity verdict
Half authored, half template. Product-specific strengths: the lead change card (Baseline → Registry now, report-section mapping, price on the button), "was Low", settings impact preview, the Review High first queue. Everything around them is the stock admin skeleton (H1 + intro + action, stats band, tabs, filter selects, table with ⋯). Both heatmaps are borrowed form: the homepage map empties when the team succeeds; the company map draws 371 cells for 2 events. The registry diff, the product's differentiator, is visible only on one card, never where triage happens.

Detector: CLI 3 advisories in ViewReport.tsx (24px h1 at :112; #c3d3db/#7f98a7 at :408), all false positives documented in DESIGN.md. Browser overlay on 4 routes at 691px: overused-font (false positive, mandated face), repeated "companies" (false positive, sr-only text), nested-cards ×4 (3 false positives; field-diff box at MonitorDetail.tsx:307 is intentional), tiny-text 11px at MonitorDetail.tsx:325/:329 (documented, but DESIGN.md contradicts itself), line-length ~88 chars at SeveritySettings.tsx:100 (real). The detector caught none of the behavioural issues. Overlay server stopped afterwards.

## What's working
1. The lead change card (MonitorDetail.tsx:293-360): judge the change, see the report section and price, decide; buying the report pre-fills Actioned.
2. The review loop: Review High first queue, position "3 of 133", Next after each review, filters in the URL, focus moves to the next feed row.
3. Honest state and impact copy: stop dialog, Order history status explanations, cadence-safe checks copy, settings impact preview.

## Priority issues
- [P1] Decisions happen without the evidence, and the evidence disappears afterwards (feed Review menu, bulk bar, change log at MonitorDetail.tsx:420+). Fix: one-line diff in feed and change-log rows, expandable full diff, inline diff before High quick-decisions, list High items with diffs in bulk review. → /impeccable clarify
- [P1] Rows and filters describe only the most severe change (verified src/data/queue.ts:53). Fix: filter on any unreviewed change, "+N more unreviewed", newest-date Detected or relabel. → /impeccable harden
- [P1] Slow for power users, keyboard focus lost: no shortcuts/range select/page size/export, focus drops to body after bulk review and report confirm, mouse-only row open, silent bulk bar. Fix: shortcut layer with "?" sheet, range select, page size + jump, Export, focus return, polite live regions. → /impeccable harden
- [P2] Three competing counts and a misnamed "unread"; heatmap blank when caught up. Fix: one hero unit (companies), change count to feed header, rename alerts, queue trend instead of heatmap. → /impeccable distill
- [P2] Paying for reports: fresh-report primary on fully reviewed companies (MonitorDetail.tsx:231), no balance in confirms, double baseline on /report/:id, unguarded Settings drafts. Fix: secondary when nothing unreviewed, balance in confirms, combined order or baseline reuse, leave guard. → /impeccable shape

## Persona red flags
- Alex: ~3 tab stops per row, no keyboard row-open, no range select, select-all hidden behind page checkbox, prev/next across 50 pages, bulk review ends with focus on body, 2 clicks per feed review with no batch, no feed search, full explainer every Create.
- Sam: unreviewed dot is a role-less span with aria-label (Monitoring.tsx:495, :584), no result-count announcements, silent bulk bar, focus loss after bulk review and Get report, green focus ring ~3.0:1 on navy bulk bar.
- Priya (payment-gateway analyst, ~1,000 merchants): Ownership sweep misses merchants, no merchant ID/segment/tier/assignee, no reviewed-by or stopped-by, no export, Checked-before-Detected dates, Taiwan offered in Search but unsupported in the dialog.

## Questions to consider
1. What if the unit of triage were the field change, with before/after visible?
2. Should the homepage surface cross-portfolio patterns (same new shareholder across merchants, liquidation clusters)?
3. Should severity rules work per field or per transition, not only per category?
4. What does this homepage look like for a team that is caught up?
